# Round 2 — Sound-first candidates (2026-08-12)

> The Round 1 recommendation (`Trove`) was rejected on **sound** ("does not
> sound good"). This addendum is a sound-first rework: short, soft, pleasant
> names that still bridge records **and** books and trace to real product
> behavior. **Vibe selection is still open** — none of these is locked until
> the user picks a direction.

## Vibe menu

| Vibe | Name | Sound | Story (grounded in product) | Collision note `[VALIDATE]` |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Cozy / warm | **Cove** | 1 syll., soft `o`+`v` | *"Your cove."* A sheltered treasure spot for crate + shelf; dark, intimate aesthetic | moderate |
| Cozy / warm | **Hearth** | 2 soft syllables | *"The hearth of your collection."* Records & books belong together at home | low–moderate |
| Cozy / warm | **Haven** | soft, open | *"A haven for your records and books."* | moderate (common word) |
| Cozy / warm | **Nest** | tiny, sticky | *"Build your nest."* | **high** — Google Nest |
| Story / collector | **Lore** | 1 syll., round `o` | *"The lore of your collection."* Records & books are the stories you keep | low–moderate |
| Story / collector | **Stash** | short, playful | *"Your stash."* Casual collector energy | moderate |
| Treasure / premium | **Vault** | deep, resonant | *"Your vault."* Fits the `#16130F` treasure mood | moderate (banking / password managers) |
| Treasure / premium | **Keep** | clean, verb+noun | *"Your keep."* A stronghold for your things | **high** — Google Keep |

## Lead recommendation

- **Primary: `Cove`** — best blend of sound, warmth, and meaning; it names a
  *place* (like "crate" and "shelf" are places), and has no heavyweight
  collision.
- **Runner-up: `Lore`** — for a sharper, story-driven edge.
