## Translation-ready notes (post-reveal)

- **Alcove** stays untranslated everywhere (proper noun).
- Per-market tone + draft lines: `copy-kit-alcove.md` §7 (FR/PT/NL/DE/ES/IT).
- Localize **the pain, not the pitch** first: the double-buy confession and the
  "do I own this?" moment are culture-proof; the wordplay ("Two loves. One
  alcove.") needs a native pass.
- For FR/NL/PT-BR/DE/ES/IT hashtags, keep `#vinyl`/`#bookstagram` (global) and
  add local ones (e.g. `#vinyles`, `#schallplatten`, `#livros`…) — `[VALIDATE]`
  with native testers.
- Never auto-translate the reveal thread without a native review — the reveal is
  the single highest-stakes post.

## Posting rhythm guardrails

- 1 hero post/day/platform max during Phases 1–4 — anticipation, not noise.
- Stories/statuses can be looser (2–4/day) but must not spoil the next phase.
- Never post the identical caption on X, IG, and FB — retune the first line
  and CTA per platform.
- Reply to every comment on Days 5–11 (curiosity phase = every reply compounds).
