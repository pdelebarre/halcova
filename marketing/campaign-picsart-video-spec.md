# PicsArt video production spec — "What lives in your halcova?"

**Owner:** Marketing Manager · **Status:** Draft · **Date:** 2026-08-13
**Tool:** PicsArt (free/paid tier — all assets below are template-friendly)
**Grounding:** visuals match the real app aesthetic (dark `#16130F`, gold
`#C9A227`, cream `#EFE6D8`, label-red `#B23A2E` for "double-buy" alarm moments;
Fraunces serif + Inter + IBM Plex Mono). No fake UI, no invented screens — demo
shots are real screen recordings of Halcova with motion/title overlays.

---

## 1. Brand kit (build once, reuse)

| Element | Value | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Background | `#16130F` (sleeve black) | the app's real body color |
| Accent | `#C9A227` (runout gold) | titles, underlines, the "scan" sweep |
| Text | `#EFE6D8` (jacket kraft / cream) | body text, captions |
| Alarm accent | `#B23A2E` (label red) | "bought it twice", "already own" moments |
| Display font | Fraunces (serif) | if missing in PicsArt, use a similar warm serif — titles |
| Body font | Inter (sans) | if missing, use a clean geometric sans — subtitles |
| Tech accent font | IBM Plex Mono → fallback to any mono | "SCANNING…", barcode labels |
| Motif | **barcode** | scan-line sweep, "beep" flash, thin black/gold bars |
| Motif 2 | **the Halcova arch + barcode** (the brand mark) | Gothic arch / treasure nook with a barcode element — see `marketing/brief-halcova-icon.md` + `docs/icon-treasure-nook-spec.md` |

**PicsArt setup:** create a **Project** called `halcova-campaign` with saved
templates (9:16, 1:1, 16:9) pre-loaded with the bg color, fonts, and the
arch + barcode logo. Every asset below starts from the template — this is how
you produce 14 videos in an afternoon.

**Master text-safety margins:** keep text inside the center 80% (Stories/Reels
hide top 15% behind UI and bottom 20% behind caption/CTA). PicsArt's safe-zone
guide is your friend.

---

## 2. Global format specs

| Platform | Ratio | Max len | Where |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Instagram Reels | 9:16 (1080×1920) | 15–60s | feed + Stories |
| Instagram Stories | 9:16 | ≤15s per segment | countdown, polls, reveals |
| Facebook Reels | 9:16 | 15–60s | feed + groups |
| Facebook feed | 1:1 (1080×1080) | 15–30s | square crop of the reel |
| X | 16:9 (1280×720) or 1:1 | 15–30s | video embeds in tweets |

**Rule of thumb:** build every asset at 9:16 with the hook in the top-center
third; export 1:1 and 16:9 crops from the same project. **Hook rule:** the first
2 seconds must contain the full question/statement in text — most viewers decide
in 1.5s, with sound off.

## 3. Asset storyboards (V1–V14)

> Text = exact on-screen copy. PicsArt tips in *italics*.

### V1 — "You've bought this record twice." (6s, 9:16) · Phase 1
- **0–2s:** black bg. Text appears: **"You've bought this record twice."** —
  *PicsArt: typewriter text animation, gold on black, serif.*
- **2–4s:** a record sleeve spins slowly (stock or a photo of your own crate).
  Text: **"Be honest."** in red.
- **4–6s:** zoom on the "twice" → cut to black. Text: **"Something is coming."**
  — *add a faint record crackle audio.*
- **Audio:** low vinyl crackle + a single soft "pop" on "Be honest."
- **No faces, no product, no name.**

### V2 — "I built something for people like us." (9s, 9:16) · Phase 1
- **0–2s:** hook text: **"300 records. No idea what I own."** *(zoom-in text)*
- **2–5s:** quick montage: crate stack, "I'll sort it later" shelf, a book spine
  close-up (3 fast cuts). Text on each: **"The crate." "The pile." "The shelf."**
- **5–7s:** black. **"So I built something."**
- **7–9s:** **"For people like us. 🕯️"** — *fade to black with a glow.*
- **Audio:** lo-fi beat or warm ambient; cuts synced to beat.

### V3 — "Point your camera at it." (9s, 9:16) · Phase 2
- **0–2s:** text hook: **"The whole trick?"**
- **2–6s:** a hand pointing a phone at a barcode (photo/video you shoot; blur the
  phone screen so no UI shows). *Add a gold scan-line sweeping across the
  barcode — PicsArt effect/sticker or simple animated bar.*
- **6–9s:** **"Point your camera at it. That's it."** — mono font, feels like a
  system message. End with a **beep** sound.
- **Audio:** room tone + a clean "beep" on the scan sweep.

### V4 — "Two loves. One place." (9s, 9:16) · Phase 2
- **0–2s:** left half shows a record, right half shows a book (split screen).
- **2–5s:** the two halves slide together into the **Halcova arch (brand mark)**
  outline. *(Use PicsArt's arch/sticker or draw the arch + barcode from the icon
  brief; animate the halves into it.)*
- **5–9s:** centered under the arch: **"Two loves. One place."** — serif, gold.
  Then small mono line: **"clue #2"**.
- **Audio:** warm pad; a vinyl crackle on the record side, a soft page-turn on
  the book side, both resolving into one chord.

### V5 — "Signs you need this" (carousel, not video) · Phase 2
- 7 static slides (copy in copy-bank Day 8), same brand kit, one icon per sign
  (reuse simple PicsArt icons: record, stack, magnifier, book, arch).

### V6 — Countdown (9s, 9:16) · Phase 2
- **0–2s:** black. Mono font: **"3"** big and gold. *(PicsArt countdown text
  animation)*
- **2–5s:** **"2"**
- **5–7s:** **"1"**
- **7–9s:** **"Tomorrow. 🌒"** (Day 10 version) or **"The name reveal."** (Day 9)
- **Audio:** rising tick, cut off at the end (cliffhanger).
- **Stories:** pair with the built-in countdown sticker (Day 9–10).

### V7 — Name reveal "Meet Halcova." (15s, 9:16) · Phase 3 ⭐
- **0–2s:** black, no text — just the crackle starting.
- **2–6s:** the Halcova arch + barcode (brand mark) draws itself in gold.
  *(PicsArt: draw-on animation or a gold arch sticker revealed by a wipe.)*
- **6–10s:** **"HALCOVA"** types in, serif, gold, letter by letter. *(PicsArt
  typewriter, slow.)*
- **10–13s:** sub-line: **"Your records and books, in their own halcova."**
- **13–15s:** **"Soon. 👀"** — *gold glow fade.*
- **Audio:** vinyl crackle resolving into a warm, confident chord at "HALCOVA";
  subtle heartbeat/rising swell under the reveal.
- **Post it at the reveal time on ALL platforms the same day** (this is the
  spike).

### V8 — Demo: scan a record (30s, 9:16) · Phase 3 ⭐
Real screen recording of Halcova (record tab), edited in PicsArt:
- **0–2s:** hook text overlay: **"The whole app in 30 seconds."**
- **2–14s:** scan a real record sleeve → match picker → confirm. Overlays:
  mono captions **"SCANNING…"**, **"FOUND IT"**, gold scan-sweep over the
  barcode. *(Screen recording zoomed to keep the phone UI readable.)*
- **14–22s:** the "not in your crate yet" flag. Caption: **"It remembers what
  you own."**
- **22–30s:** added to crate. End card: **"Your crate. Cataloged."**
- **Audio:** the app's real beep/vibration sound if any, else a clean beep +
  light beat. Keep it ASMR-satisfying.
- **Never fabricate the UI.** This is a real recording; if the screen is messy,
  re-shoot rather than fake it.

### V9 — Demo: scan a book (30s, 9:16) · Phase 3
- Same structure as V8 on the Books tab: **"SCANNING…"** → ISBN → edition →
  **"Already on your shelf?"** → added. End card: **"Your shelf. Cataloged."**
- **Audio:** softer — page-turn under the beep.

### V10 — "Never double-buy" (20s, 9:16) · Phase 3
- **0–3s:** reenact the used-store moment (your hand picking up a sleeve).
  Text: **"At the record shop."**
- **3–8s:** cut to the app flag: **"This exact record is already yours."** (real
  screen recording of F-07, red accent).
- **8–14s:** **"Different pressing? It tells you that too."** (the "other
  pressings you own" panel).
- **14–20s:** end card: **"Never rebuy what you already own."** — gold serif.
- **Audio:** record-fair ambience (light chatter) dropping out when the flag
  appears, replaced by a single clean "ding".

### V11 — The Open "It's open." (20s, 9:16) · Phase 4
- **0–2s:** hook: **"It's open."** *(big, gold, quick zoom)*
- **2–10s:** fast cut of V8/V9's best 4 seconds each (scan → flag → added).
- **10–16s:** text: **"Your crate. Your shelf. One halcova."**
- **16–20s:** **"Request access →"** with the link/QR. *(Stories: swipe-up/
  link sticker.)*
- **Audio:** triumphant, warm; end on a solid chord.

### V12 — UGC challenge "Show us your halcova." (15s, 9:16) · Phase 5
- **0–3s:** hook: **"New challenge."**
- **3–10s:** montage of real user scans (permissioned) — *or* a stylized
  re-enactment until you have real ones.
- **10–15s:** **"Post your scan with #WhatsInYourHalcova. Best ones get
  reposted."**
- **Audio:** trending/upbeat; keep captions on (many watch muted).

### V13 — Brand bumper (6s, 9:16 + 1:1) · all phases
- The arch + barcode draws itself → **HALCOVA** → **"two loves, one halcova."**
- Used as an end-card or Stories bumper on every post (consistency = recall).

### V14 — "How it works" explainer (60s, 16:9 for X / 9:16 for IG) · Phase 4+
- Longer cut of V8 + V9 + V10: scan record, scan book, never-double-buy, family
  members each get their own crate/shelf (the real multi-user flow F-21–F-24,
  shown as "family and friends can join — their own private crate and shelf").
  Chapter titles in mono font.
- **Post:** X pinned, IG as a "highlight"/saved-story, FB as a longer Reel.

## 4. PicsArt techniques cheat-sheet

| Effect | Use for | How (PicsArt) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Scan-line sweep | barcode moments (V3, V8, V9) | animated gold bar / glitch effect over the barcode |
| Typewriter text | reveals (V7, name) | text animation preset |
| Countdown | V6, Stories | countdown sticker + manual numbers |
| Split-screen | V4 (record/book) | split effect; animate halves together |
| Draw-on / wipe | Halcova arch + barcode (V4, V7, V13) | draw animation or a shaped mask wipe |
| Glow fade | cliffhangers (V1, V2, V7) | opacity/glow keyframe at the end |
| Zoom text | hooks (V2, V11) | scale-in text on the hook line |
| Color grade | all | keep `#16130F` blacks, gold accents — consistent brand |
| Music/audio | all | one saved "campaign track" style; keep it low (captions carry the message) |

**Caption/subtitle rule:** every Reel must work **muted** — all key text is
on-screen, short, in the safe zone.

## 5. Production checklist (before Day 1)

- [ ] Brand kit saved as a PicsArt Project + 3 ratio templates (9:16, 1:1, 16:9).
- [ ] Record your own footage: a crate, a shelf, a hand scanning a barcode, a
      used-store sleeve moment (10–20 clips, phone, good light).
- [ ] Real screen recordings: scan a record (V8), scan a book (V9), the
      already-own flag (V10), multi-user note (V14) — record with a second
      device or phone screen recorder.
- [ ] Audio assets: a clean "beep", record crackle, page turn, a campaign music
      bed (PicsArt library is fine).
- [ ] Build V13 (bumper) first — it defines the look; reuse it everywhere.
- [ ] Build the Phase-1 assets (V1–V3, carousel) this week; Phase 3 assets
      (V7–V10) only after the real screen recordings + rename are ready.
- [ ] A 2-second "hook slide" at the top of every video (text-only, gold on
      black) — the non-negotiable attention gate.
