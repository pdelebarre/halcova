# YouTube channel playbook — Halcova launch

**Owner:** Marketing Manager · **Status:** Draft · **Date:** 2026-08-13
**Channel role:** the **search + long-tail reach** channel. YouTube is where the
campaign's videos live on after the feed scrolls past — Shorts bring new,
non-following viewers (the "record fair" and "bookshelf" search crowd), and
long-form gives the product a permanent home people can find for months.
**Brand:** Halcova · **Grounding:** every title/description/joke traces to real
behavior (`F-01…F-25`); benefit bank + humor voice from
`review-benefits-humor.md` §4–§5. **Do-not-say** (reuse): no access codes
(`RU-…`), no admin/approval internals, no app-store/pricing claims, no fake
testimonials, counts, or metrics.

**Current platform facts (sources, 2025):** YouTube Shorts are 9:16 vertical,
up to **3 minutes** since Oct 2024, MP4/MOV — but Shorts **over 60s can't use
the audio picker** (no copyrighted music), so campaign assets stay ≤60s
(Hootsuite, "YouTube Shorts: Everything you need to know in 2025", July 2025).
Shorts reach new audiences **without existing subscribers**, autoplay + loop,
and the **first 2–3 seconds decide the swipe**; Shorts also **ramp slower** than
Reels/TikTok (often a couple of days before a hit) — don't judge an asset after
24h. Titles should be **specific and SEO-aware**, not vague or baity. Global
Shorts volume was ~**200B views/day** in 2025 (YouTube blog).

## 1. Where YouTube fits the funnel

```
Short feed / YouTube search  →  channel page  →  (Subscribe)
   ↓  UTM in description / end screen
Landing (utm_source=youtube)  →  Request access  →  approve → sign in → scan
```

- **Shorts = the top of funnel.** Same 9:16 assets as IG Reels (V1–V12), so the
  marginal cost is near zero — one edit pass, one extra upload per day.
- **Long-form = the middle + retention.** From Phase 4 on, 3–5 min videos that
  are searchable ("how to catalog your record collection", "never double-buy")
  and give the landing a video to embed. These are the assets that still bring
  views in month 2.
- **Playlists = the loop.** "The reveal", "How it works", "Never double-buy",
  "Community crates" — playlists raise watch time and feed the next video.

## 4. Shorts production & publishing rules

- **Re-cut, don't re-upload:** take the 9:16 IG Reel export, re-tune the hook
  text for YouTube, add a **#Shorts** tag. Never paste an identical asset with
  no changes — YouTube and IG reward different hooks.
- **Hook in the first 2 seconds on screen** (sound off) — same rule as the
  video spec §2, non-negotiable.
- **Design for the loop:** end on a visual/text beat that makes the replay
  satisfying (e.g. the "already yours" flag appearing — rewatch value). Loops
  drive retention, which is the main Shorts signal.
- **Length:** ≤60s (keeps the music picker + loop-friendly). All V1–V12 assets
  already are.
- **Resolution:** 9:16, export at 1080×1920, MP4/MOV.
- **Don't expect instant results:** Shorts can take a couple of days to ramp —
  re-check the same asset after 48–72h before deciding it failed.
- **Verification:** verify the channel (phone/YouTube Studio) so you can set
  **custom thumbnails** on Shorts too.
- **Remix your own library:** clip the best 4 seconds of V8/V9/V10 as standalone
  Shorts later — free repackaging of the launch.

## 6. UTM & tracking

`https://<halcova-domain>/?utm_source=youtube&utm_medium=social&utm_campaign=halcova-launch-2026&utm_content=<asset>`

| Where the link goes | utm_content |
| --- | --- |
| Channel description + banner | `channel-page` |
| V7 reveal / Long "Meet Halcova" | `v7-reveal` |
| V8 / V9 / V10 demos | `v8-demo` / `v9-demo` / `v10-never-rebuy` |
| V11 open / Long "How it works" | `v11-open` / `how-it-works` |
| Challenge V12 | `v12-challenge` |
| Long "Why collectors double-buy" | `double-buy-story` |

Only put the link in the description (and a card on long-form). Never put a
request link in a teaser Short before Phase 4 — no live access gate yet.

**Analytics to watch (YouTube Studio + landing):**
- Shorts: views, **average % viewed** (retention), rewatches, "swiped away",
  subs gained per Short, **traffic sources** (Shorts feed vs search).
- Long-form: watch time, **impressions CTR** (thumbnail + title test),
  **traffic sources: YouTube search** (the SEO KPI), subscribers.
- Landing: visits with `utm_source=youtube` → **request access** → approved →
  first scan (the funnel in `campaign-viral-launch.md` §7).
- **Geography:** YouTube Analytics geography shows where the collector audience
  is — feed the localization decision (which markets get localized videos
  first).

## 8. Risks & mitigations

| # | Risk | Mitigation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| R-Y1 | **YouTube spoils the reveal** — Shorts auto-titles/suggested content leak the name before Day 11 | Phases 1–2 Shorts are name-free (titles from §5 have no "Halcova"); don't add the brand hashtag until the reveal; keep V7 scheduled for Day 11 exactly. |
| R-Y2 | Shorts judged after 24h and killed early | Shorts ramp over a couple of days — re-check at 72h before pulling an asset. |
| R-Y3 | Copy-paste IG asset gets weak Shorts performance | Re-tune hook text + title per YouTube (the two feeds favor different hooks). |
| R-Y4 | Comments leak internals or the access model | Pin a safe question per Short; reply thread stays public-safe (no codes, no approval talk). |
| R-Y5 | Channel grows but doesn't convert | Every description + end screen carries the single UTM'd "Request access" link; measure landing funnel per asset. |
| R-Y6 | Monetization expectations | Not monetizing at launch (a PWA, no paid tier to promise) — no revenue claims anywhere. |
