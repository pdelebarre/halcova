## 1. Concept

> **"What lives in your halcova?"**

A three-week, four-phase **progressive-reveal** campaign. We never show the
product in the first week. We make the audience feel the problem, guess the
solution, and beg for the name — then we hand it to them, let them in, and turn
them into the ones who tell the next person.

**Why curiosity-first works for this product:**
- Halcova has a **built-in mystery** (the name) and a **built-in reveal** (scan →
  it knows what you own).
- Its two strongest hooks — "never double-buy" (duplicate detection, F-07) and
  "your crate and your shelf in one place" — are **relatable pains first, product
  features second**. Sell the pain, then reveal the cure.
- The access model ("request access", F-21) is real **scarcity** — a perfect
  complement to a curiosity campaign. Never leak codes publicly, but the *gate*
  itself is the hook.

**Info-control ladder (the heart of the plan):**

| Phase | Audience knows | Audience does NOT know yet | Audience wants |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 — The Riddle (Days 1–5) | a painful truth (double-buying, un-cataloged crates) + "something is coming" | what it is, the name, the platform | to follow so they don't miss it |
| 2 — The Clues (Days 6–10) | it catalogs records **and** books; it uses your camera; it's "a place" | the name, the look, the brand | to guess, to get in |
| 3 — The Reveal (Days 11–13) | **Halcova**; first real demo; "never double-buy" | how to get in | access |
| 4 — The Open (Day 14+) | it's live; request access; WhatsApp circle gets codes | (scarcity) | to be inside |
| 5 — The Loop (ongoing) | everything — now they're the ones sharing | — | to show theirs |

## 2. Positioning & the angle (what we're really selling)

- **One line:** Halcova catalogs your records and books by scanning their
  barcodes, remembers what you already own so you never double-buy, and keeps
  your crate and your shelf in one cozy place.
- **Emotional angle:** "You love these things. Keep them straight. Stop
  rebuying them."
- **Competitive angle (grounded, directional):**
  - *Books:* Goodreads (Amazon) is the giant for *reading* and "to-read" shelves,
    not for cataloging a physical library by ISBN with covers & editions —
    confirmed at goodreads.com/about (launched 2007, ~20B recommendation data
    points; it's a social-reading platform). Halcova's angle: **catalog your
    physical shelf**, not your reading list.
  - *Records:* Discogs is the database & marketplace; its tools are
    catalogue-nerd oriented. Halcova's angle: **camera-first, consumer-friendly
    "crate" for the person who owns 200 records and just wants them organized**,
    plus the never-double-buy guard at the record shop.
  - *Both at once:* nobody owns "crate + shelf in one cozy place." That is the
    white space this campaign claims. `[VALIDATE]` before quoting competitors by
    name in paid/press copy.
- **Guardrails (from `copy-kit-halcova.md` §8):** never mention access codes
  (`RU-…`), the admin key, or approval mechanics publicly; never claim app-store
  availability or pricing; never invent testimonials, counts, or metrics.

## 3. Phase-by-phase plan

### Phase 1 — The Riddle (Days 1–5) · *"You've bought this twice. Be honest."*

Goal: build an audience of people who feel the pain. **No product, no name.**

- **Content pillars (1 post/day × 3 platforms, + Stories):**
  1. The double-buy confession hook (video V1–V2).
  2. The un-cataloged crate ("300 records, no idea what you own").
  3. The shelf-shaming post ("books you forgot you owned").
  4. The "does anyone else?" question post (comment-bait).
  5. The promise: "I built something for people like us. Soon." (follow-bait).
- **Viral lever:** relatability + a question in every post. Comments are the
  fuel; each comment boosts reach.
- **CTA:** Follow / "save this if it's you." No links.
- **WhatsApp:** silent here. Statuses mirror the teasers *without* codes.

### Phase 2 — The Clues (Days 6–10) · *"Two loves. One place."*

Goal: reveal the *concept* (not the product) and start a guessing game.

- **Content pillars:**
  1. Camera-pointing-at-barcode motion piece — no UI shown (video V3–V5).
  2. Crate + shelf silhouette piece ("Two loves. One place.") (V4).
  3. The "signs you need this" carousel (IG) / thread (X) — saveable.
  4. **Guess-the-name game** — "comment what you'd call it" (comment-bait).
  5. **Countdown to the reveal** (Stories countdown sticker; V6).
- **Viral lever:** the guessing game + countdown = repeat visits, DMs, and
  anticipation. DM volume is your best Phase-2 signal.
- **CTA:** "DM the word CRATE" (IG) / reply to the thread (X) — engagement DMs
  boost you hard. No links yet.

### Phase 3 — The Reveal (Days 11–13) · *"Meet Halcova."*

Goal: the spike. Name + first real demo. **This is the phase gated on the
Hokan→Halcova code rename + legal + icon.**

- **Content:**
  1. **Name-reveal hero video** (V7) — the wordmark, dark + gold, two loves.
  2. **Demo #1 — scan a record** (V8, the money shot): scan → confirm → "not in
     your crate yet" → added.
  3. **Demo #2 — scan a book** (V9): ISBN → shelf.
  4. **"Never double-buy"** beat (V10): the used-store moment, diff pressing flag.
  5. X: the reveal **thread** (pain → concept → name → demo → what's next).
- **Viral lever:** the reveal itself is shareable; demo = saveable; "tag the
  friend who buys records twice" (share-bait). This is the day to cross-post
  everywhere and (optional) run a small paid boost to the hero video.
- **CTA:** "Follow — it opens soon." No links yet (access gates not public).

### Phase 4 — The Open (Day 14+) · *"It's open. Request access."*

Goal: convert curiosity into members. **Public landing goes live this day.**

- **Content:**
  1. **Open announcement** (V11): "Halcova is open. Request access." → link.
  2. Demo reminders (recycle V8–V10 with new captions + UTM).
  3. **WhatsApp personal invitations go out here** — personal codes, 1:1
     (see `campaign-whatsapp-playbook.md`), NOT public.
  4. First real user proof — *only real, with permission* (see Phase 5).
- **Viral lever:** scarcity ("requests open in waves"), plus the invite model
  ("your crate and shelf, and your family can have theirs" — the real multi-user
  flow, F-24).
- **CTA:** link with `utm_source` per channel → landing → **Request access**.

### Phase 5 — The Loop (ongoing) · *"Show us your halcova."*

Goal: users become the channel. **This is where real virality compounds.**

- **Content:**
  1. **UGC challenge:** "Catalog your collection — show the before/after."
  2. **Repost/duet/stitch** real user scans (with permission) — never fabricate.
  3. **The "double-buy story" ask:** "tell us the record you own twice" —
     engagement + stories fuel.
  4. **Transformation posts:** "my crate, digitized" (real, permissioned).
  5. Weekly **community Q&A / tips** posts to keep the account alive between
     launches.
- **Viral lever:** shares, saves, and user tagging. Your real-user content (even
  a handful of people) outperforms any produced ad for trust.

## 5. The curiosity mechanics (why this escalates)

1. **Withhold the name.** The name is the reveal. Anyone who guesses or waits
   feels invested before they ever see the product.
2. **Sell the pain before the cure.** Every teaser ends on a feeling, not a
   feature.
3. **End every post with a question or a cliffhanger.** Comments and DMs are the
   algorithm fuel and your real interest signal.
4. **Escalate the ask:** follow → save → comment → DM → request access → invite
   a friend. Each step is bigger than the last and only unlocked by the previous.
5. **Tight timeline.** Curiosity decays. Teaser window is **10 days max** —
   don't string people along for a month.
6. **Scarcity is real, so use it honestly:** access is approval-gated (F-21).
   Frame it as "request access" and open in waves — never fake urgency.
7. **Hand it to them.** The most shareable moment of the whole campaign is
   Phase 5, when real people show real crates. Design for that from Day 1.

## 7. Measurement, KPIs & risks

### Funnel & events (define in analytics before Day 14)

```
Impressions → Video views (3s+) → Profile visits → Landing visits (UTM)
→ Request access submitted → Approved → First sign-in (code) → First scan
→ First item added → Returns in week 2 (retention) → Invites a friend (loop)
```

### KPIs (targets are starting points — recalibrate after Phase 1)

| Stage | Metric | Phase-1 target (organic) | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Reach | impressions / views | 1–3k/asset | calibrate in week 1 |
| Engagement | comments + DMs per post | ≥ 3% of reach | DM volume is the signal |
| Curiosity | guesses / DM "CRATE" | ≥ 20 DMs by Phase 2 end | the name-reveal appetite |
| Funnel | landing visits → requests | ≥ 10% | measure with UTM |
| Activation | requests → first scan | ≥ 50% | admin approves in waves |
| Retention | week-2 return | ≥ 30% | the real test |
| Brand | "halcova app" branded search % | trending up | the brand-search KPI (grows after the reveal) |
| Loop | shares per post; UGC entries | growing | Phase 5 compounding |
| YouTube reach | Shorts views / avg % viewed | 1–3k views, ≥60% avg viewed | Shorts ramp over 48–72h — judge at 3 days |
| YouTube funnel | Short/long → landing (UTM) → requests | ≥ 5% request click-through from YT visits | measure via `utm_source=youtube` |
| YouTube SEO | long-form views from search | growing | "how to catalog your collection" search crowd |

### UTM scheme (one link per channel, per post)
`https://<halcova-domain>/?utm_source=<x|instagram|facebook|whatsapp|youtube>&utm_medium=<social|status|chat>&utm_campaign=halcova-launch-2026&utm_content=<V#|post-slug>`

### Risks & mitigations

| # | Risk | Mitigation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| R-1 | Hokan→Halcova code rename + legal/icon gates not green before the reveal | Phases 1–2 run name-free (safe now); block Phase 3 on the gates (§0). |
| R-2 | Teaser fatigue (too much hype, no payoff) | Hard cap teaser window at 10 days; reveal on schedule even if imperfect; keep 1 strong post/day, not 5. |
| R-3 | **Access approval is manual** — a viral wave could swamp the admin panel (F-23) | Open **in waves**; message "request access — opening in batches"; have the owner ready to batch-approve; if it truly breaks, pause Phase 4 amplification and reopen when stable. This is a real product constraint — flag to Netlify Backend for a waitlist auto-approve later. |
| R-4 | Engagement-bait penalties (IG/FB down-rank "comment YES" bait) | Ask *genuine* questions ("how many have you bought twice?") — real engagement, not fake bait. |
| R-5 | Copy-paste cross-posting feels lazy / algorithmic suppression | Tune captions + first lines per platform (§4). |
| R-6 | Claiming features we don't have (store listings, pricing, metrics) | Copy bank is grounded in real behavior (F-01…F-24); do-not-say list in `copy-kit-halcova.md` §8. Never invent testimonials. |
| R-7 | International backlash / "Halcova" meaning or sound in a target language | A native-speaker check for **Halcova** must pass before localized reveals `[VALIDATE]`. |
| R-8 | WhatsApp feels like spam to contacts | Personal, sequenced, opt-in feeling — playbook file. Never mass-broadcast, never send codes to group chats/statuses. |
| R-9 | **YouTube spoils the reveal** — Shorts leak the name before Day 11 | Phases 1–2 Shorts stay name-free; V7 scheduled exactly for Day 11 (playbook R-Y1). |
| R-10 | Shorts judged after 24h and killed early | Shorts ramp over a couple of days — re-check at 72h before pulling (playbook R-Y2). |
